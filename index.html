Built a 10-floor hotel elevator simulation in C++ with human and AI play modes. The AI uses an anger-weighted greedy dispatch algorithm — scoring every floor by cumulative passenger frustration, routing the closest idle elevator, and optimizing each pickup by loading whichever direction group (up vs. down) carries the higher combined anger to maximize satisfaction per trip. Engineered the full simulation engine: per-tick elevator movement, anger escalation, passenger explosions, a move-command parser, and save/load support.
